# Submission checklist

- Obtain and insert the exact UWM ethics/IRB or not-human-subjects determination.
- Confirm title, author order, affiliations, corresponding author, grant number, and no-competing-interests statement with every author.
- Confirm the target ISBI 2027 template, page limit, anonymity policy, AI-assistance policy, and deadline when the official call is released.
- Decide whether the conference version will center the acquisition-dependent result or only the fluorescence result.
- Keep patient-grouped TNBC results labelled exploratory because labels were viewed before this study.
- Do not describe literature tables as direct rankings; protocols and metrics differ.
- Do not claim clinical validation, cancer diagnosis, instance segmentation, or proven 3-D superiority.
- Archive the final commit hash, environment, data hashes, fixed folds, and checkpoints used for the submitted numbers.
- Run the complete test and result-validation commands documented in the repository before export.
- Have all authors inspect every figure, table, citation, and numerical claim against the saved outputs.
