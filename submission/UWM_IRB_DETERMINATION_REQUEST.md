# UWM IRB determination request — text for PI submission

This document is a preparation aid, not an IRB determination. The PI must submit the request through UWM I-Manager or send the requested information to the UWM IRB. Use the exact title and facts below only after PI verification.

## Proposed title

Public-data evaluation of edge-enhanced 2D nuclear segmentation across fluorescence microscopy and breast histology

## Short project description

We develop and evaluate computational methods for semantic segmentation of cell nuclei in existing microscopy images. The public experiments use BBBC039/BBBC038 cell-line fluorescence benchmarks and TNBC Nuclei Segmentation v1.1, a previously published public histopathology dataset. The work involves no recruitment, interaction, intervention, prospective specimen collection or access to medical records.

## Human-data source

- Dataset: TNBC Nuclei Segmentation v1.1
- Public record: https://zenodo.org/records/2579118
- Original publication: P. Naylor et al., IEEE Transactions on Medical Imaging, 2019, DOI 10.1109/TMI.2018.2865709
- Data used: 50 released H&E image fields, binary nuclear masks and dataset-provided patient-group prefixes for grouped statistical aggregation
- Access: unrestricted public download under CC BY 4.0

## Identifiability and contact

The investigators receive no names, medical-record numbers, dates of birth, contact information, facial images, genomic data, clinical records or linkage key. The image fields and masks cannot be linked by the investigators to an identified person. Investigators will not attempt re-identification and will not contact any source individual. Only provider-supplied group prefixes are used to avoid treating multiple images from the same source as independent observations.

## Requested determination

Please determine whether this secondary analysis of public, de-identified image data constitutes research not involving human subjects under UWM policy and 45 CFR 46.102(e), or whether another UWM review category applies. We request a written determination suitable for citation in an IEEE ISBI manuscript.
