## Reviewer 2 and EMBC concerns

The draft focuses its introduction on the empirical question, adds directly relevant segmentation literature, defines the nucleus-foreground target, provides exact dataset/task descriptions, removes quantum framing and synthetic science illustrations, and distinguishes method components through controlled comparisons. Numerical tables come from measured outputs. Six modern matched-budget architecture baselines and five directly relevant BBBC039/TNBC literature comparisons are now included. Unrelated EEG, depression or temporal-text papers suggested in a review are not added as token citations: relevance must be justified scientifically. Histopathology classification papers may motivate the wider application but are not segmentation baselines.

The defensible contribution is now a controlled empirical finding, not a new-SOTA claim: the edge bundle gives a small gain over vanilla B7, matches rather than beats the strongest architecture baseline, and exposes polarity as a dominant transfer variable. This is scientifically stronger than the rejected claim but may be less attractive to a novelty-first methods track. The largest remaining blockers are the formal ethics determination, immutable public release, and genuinely unseen multi-center histology validation.
